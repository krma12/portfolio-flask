About me

A little about my story

I'm studying Artificial Intelligence Engineering and building my skills through hands-on projects: a Flask task manager, a Space Invaders clone, a data analysis of crime incidence in Mexico, and a real-time face filter with OpenCV.

My goal is to combine AI with cybersecurity and become a specialist in that field. Right now I'm working through Python for data, then machine learning.

Before code, I studied veterinary medicine, lived in the Mexican Caribbean and New York, opened a restaurant and became a bilingual interpreter.
